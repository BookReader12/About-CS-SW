Hardware Cleaning and Maintenance

  • Dust slows down parts becuase it traps heat
  • Use compressed air, not vacuums
  • Clean with high-purity alcohol
  • Thermal paste dries out, so it needs to be replaced
  • Unplug everything first before opening a PC
