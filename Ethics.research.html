ScreenTime For Kids

Common Technology Used; Phones, TVs, Computers

Issues

  • Technology emits blue light , which can damage the retina.
  • Causes long-term vision problems.(Macular degeneration, cataracts)
  • Can cause blurred vision, dry eyes, neck pain, back pain, headaches etc.
  • Mimics daylight.(Disrupts melatonin and sleep paterns)
  • Higher risk of becoming obese, and developing diabetes.

Ethical Issue

  • As time goes on technology is being designed to be more and more addictive. Features like endless scrolling and active notifications grab kids attention so companies can make money off of it. This conflicts with companies gaining profit and the well-being of users, as screentime addiction come with so many bad effects. At the same time , making technology so addictive means kids stay on it longer. Also meaning companies profiting off of users data just to hit them with ads and certain content to hold their focus. It's an endless cycle and will always continue as long as the world doesn't know what technology does to us and how to keep it on track.

Who does this affect?

  • Children and teens.
  • Young adults in the middle
  • Affects children and teens the most as they are still growing and adapting.

How we can prevent these issues

  • Wear blue light glasses.
  • Don't watch screens and hour before bed.
  • Reduce brightness.
  • Stay at arms length.
  • Keep tech limited and educational for young children.
  • Social tech use isn't bad, just needs to be limites and monitered.
